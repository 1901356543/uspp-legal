1. Sign in

  • Enter the server address provided by your DSS administrator.
  • Enter your username and password.
  • Tap Log in.
  • If you do not have an account, contact your DSS system administrator.

2. Monitoring center

  • View live video from connected cameras.
  • Play back recorded video by date and time.
  • Switch between camera groups and layouts.

3. Event center

  • Receive alarm push notifications.
  • View and manage event records.
  • Filter events by type, time, and device.

4. Intelligent search & Access management

  • Search events by face, person, or vehicle.
  • Manage visitor passing records.
  • Control access permissions.

5. Face Detection

The app supports face detection for security monitoring and access control. Face data is processed and stored on your own DSS server, and is not shared with third parties. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions (Location & Notifications)

  • Location: used for map display and device positioning.
  • Notifications: used for alarm push notifications.

7. Common issues

  • Cannot log in: verify the server address, username, and password with your administrator.
  • No live view: check your network connection and camera status.
  • No push notifications: check notification permissions in Settings.

8. Privacy

For information about how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
