1. Sign in

  • Enter your registered email and password.
  • Tap Log in.
  • If you forgot your password, tap Forgot password.
  • If you do not have an account, contact your project administrator.

2. Managing work orders

  • Tap a work order to view details.
  • Update status, add notes, and upload photos.
  • Submit for review when the work is complete.

3. Face Capture

Some features require face capture for identity verification or attendance purposes. Face data is processed solely for this purpose and is not shared with third parties. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions (Camera & Location)

  • Camera: required to capture photos for work orders and face verification.
  • Location: used to record the location of work orders.

5. Common issues

  • Login error: verify your email and password, and ensure network connectivity.
  • Cannot upload photos: check camera permissions in Settings.
  • App crashes: restart the app and ensure you are on the latest version.

6. Privacy

For information about how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy。
