Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the DSS Agile 8 app operated by CA M&E Engineering Pte Ltd (the "Service Provider").


About the App

DSS Agile 8 is a mobile client for DSS products (version 8.8.0 and above), including DSS Professional, DSS Express, and DSS7116D/DR-S2. It provides monitoring center, event center, intelligent search, and access management features.


Information We Collect
  • Account Information: Username, password, and user ID for authentication.
  • Video Data: Live view and video playback from connected cameras.
  • Face Data: Face images captured for face detection and recognition in security monitoring.
  • Location Data: Used for map display and device positioning.
  • Event Data: Alarm notifications and event records.
  • Visitor Data: Visitor passing records and access management.
  • Device Information: Mobile operating system and device model.

How We Use Face Data

Face data is used solely for:

  • Face detection and recognition in security monitoring.
  • Access control and visitor management.

We do not share face data with any third parties. Face data is processed and stored on the customer's own DSS server, not on our servers. Face data is retained according to the customer's configuration and applicable law.


How We Use Other Information
  • To provide live view, playback, and monitoring features.
  • To send alarm push notifications.
  • To manage access control and visitor records.
  • To provide map and device positioning.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law. All data is processed on the customer's own DSS deployment.


Data Storage

All data (including video, face data, and event records) is stored on the customer's own DSS server. We do not store this data on our own servers.


Data Retention

Data retention is controlled by the customer's DSS configuration and applicable law. Customers can configure retention periods in their DSS system.


Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact your DSS system administrator or the Service Provider at support@ca-me.com.sg.


Permissions
  • Location: used for map display.
  • Notifications: used for alarm push notifications.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age.


Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls.


Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.


Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@ca-me.com.sg.
