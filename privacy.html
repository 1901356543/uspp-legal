Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the USPP Mobile app operated by CA M&E Engineering Pte Ltd (the "Service Provider").


Information We Collect
  • Account Information: Name, Email, User ID, Phone (provided by your organisation).
  • Face Data: Face photos captured through the in-app camera for identity verification and attendance purposes.
  • Work Order Data: Descriptions, photos, and notes submitted through the app.
  • Location Data: Used to record the location of work orders.

How We Use Face Data

Face data is used solely for:

  • Identity verification of authorised personnel.
  • Attendance and access tracking.

We do not share face data with any third parties. Face data is stored on secure servers in Singapore and retained for a maximum of 12 months, after which it is permanently deleted.


How We Use Other Information
  • To provide and operate the app.
  • To manage work orders and dispatch.
  • To communicate with you about your account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law.


Data Retention
  • Account data: retained while your account is active.
  • Face data: maximum 12 months, then permanently deleted.
  • Work order data: retained as required by law.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact us at support@ca-me.com.sg.


Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age.


Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls.


Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.


Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@ca-me.com.sg.
